Try the furniture support demo

These examples use sample policies. Confirm actual order terms with the seller.

How do I confirm a delivery estimate?

Ask the seller for an estimate based on the item and your delivery ZIP code. Confirm the delivery window with the seller before purchasing.

What should I do if furniture arrives damaged?

Keep the packaging, take photos of the item and the box, and contact the seller’s support team for next steps.

Who handles returns for custom orders?

Contact the seller’s support team. In this demo, custom orders require staff review. Ask the seller to confirm eligibility and any deadlines for your order.

For furniture bought from a third-party seller, contact that seller for delivery, damage, returns, and warranty claims. Read the third-party purchase notice.
