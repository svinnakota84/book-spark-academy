From preview to course completion

Start with a public preview. The course library, assessments, and certificates require paid access.

  1. Sign in and choose an option. When enrollment opens, use your email to sign in and choose monthly membership or an individual module in Stripe Checkout.
  2. Open My Learning. After payment clears, return with the same sign-in. Monthly members see all six modules. An individual purchase unlocks the selected module.
  3. Complete the module. Each introductory module has one chaptered video, written material, and a practice exercise. Mark the lesson complete when you finish.
  4. Pass the BookSpark assessment. Score at least four out of five to earn a BookSpark Certificate of Completion. This is a BookSpark credential for an introductory module. Oracle certification is offered separately by Oracle University.

Enrollment, private playback, and certificate issuing are not live yet. Free previews are open.
