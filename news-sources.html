Automatic checks

The scheduled job checks that the recorded reuse language is present, the item is recent, the article link stays on the approved publisher’s domain and path, and the feed headline matches the publisher’s page. It skips mismatches, unavailable items, and items carrying separate rights metadata. It publishes original headlines, dates, source names, and links. Article text, photos, and AI-written summaries are excluded.

A match confirms what the publisher reports. It does not independently verify every claim. Follow the original link for corrections and updates. Email rights or accuracy concerns to legal@radiantsparkllc.com.
